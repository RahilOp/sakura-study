# Eliot, Yeats, Larkin

## Key Topics
- VII 1. T.S. Eliot - *The Love Song of J. Alfred Prufrock
- 2. W.B. Yeats - *The Lake Isle of Innisfree
- 3. Philip Larkin - *Church Going

## Definitions
- **The Love Song of J. Alfred Prufrock**: Eliot's modernist dramatic monologue on indecision.
- **The Lake Isle of Innisfree**: Yeats's Romantic escape poem.
- **Church Going**: Larkin's ironic meditation on the decline of religious practice.
