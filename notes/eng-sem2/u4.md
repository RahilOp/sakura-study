# Shakespeare, Milton, Donne, Pope

## Key Topics
- IV 1. William Shakespeare – *Let Me Not to the Marriage of
- True Minds (Sonnet No. 116) Or
- John Milton - *On His Blindness
- 2. John Donne - *Presence in Absence
- 3. Alexander Pope - *Essay on Man

## Definitions
- **Sonnet 116**: Shakespeare's sonnet on the constancy of true love.
- **On His Blindness**: Milton's Petrarchan sonnet on accepting God's will.
- **Present in Absence**: Donne's metaphysical poem on love across distance.
- **An Essay on Man**: Pope's philosophical poem in heroic couplets on human nature.
