# Gray, Wordsworth, Keats

## Key Topics
- V 1. Thomas Gray - * Elegy Written in a Country Churchyard
- 2. William Wordsworth -* The World is Too Much with Us
- 3. John Keats -* Ode on a Grecian Urn

## Definitions
- **Elegy Written in a Country Churchyard**: Gray's meditation on death and equality.
- **The World is Too Much with Us**: Wordsworth's Petrarchan sonnet critiquing materialism.
- **Ode on a Grecian Urn**: Keats's ode on art, beauty and truth.
