# Stanza forms

## Key Topics
- 1. The Heroic Couplet
- 2. Blank Verse
- 3. The Spenserian Stanza
- 4. Terza Rima

## Definitions
- **Heroic couplet**: two rhyming lines in iambic pentameter.
- **Blank verse**: unrhymed iambic pentameter.
- **Spenserian stanza**: nine lines, eight iambic pentameter + one alexandrine; rhyme ABABBCBCC.
- **Terza rima**: three-line stanzas with interlocking rhyme (aba bcb cdc…).
