# Tennyson, E.B. Browning, Arnold, R. Browning

## Key Topics
- VI 1. Alfred Lord Tennyson - *Break, Break, Break Or
- Elizabeth Barret Browning - *How Do I Love Thee?
- 2. Matthew Arnold - *Dover Beach
- 3. Robert Browning - *My Last Duchess

## Definitions
- **Break, Break, Break**: Tennyson's elegiac lyric on loss.
- **How Do I Love Thee?**: E.B. Browning's sonnet counting the ways of love.
- **Dover Beach**: Arnold's poem on the loss of religious faith.
- **My Last Duchess**: Browning's dramatic monologue of a possessive duke.
