## Definitions
- **Metre**: pattern of stressed and unstressed syllables.
- **Iamb**: unstressed + stressed syllable (da-DUM).
- **Trochee**: stressed + unstressed syllable (DUM-da).
- **Simile**: comparison using 'like' or 'as'.
- **Metaphor**: direct comparison without 'like/as'.
- **Personification**: giving human qualities to non-human things.
- **Onomatopoeia**: words that imitate sounds.
- **Irony**: saying the opposite of what is meant, or outcome contrary to expectation.
- **Negative capability**: Keats's term for accepting uncertainty without rushing to reason.
