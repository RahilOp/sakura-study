- 5. The Ballad

- 7. The Dramatic Monologue
- 8. Allegory

## Definitions
- **Sonnet**: 14-line lyric poem, usually iambic pentameter.
- **Elegy**: a poem of lament, often mourning death.
- **Ode**: a formal, celebratory lyric addressed to a person or thing.
- **Epic**: long narrative poem about heroic deeds.
- **Ballad**: short narrative folk song, often with dialogue.
- **Lyric**: short poem expressing a single emotion.
- **Dramatic monologue**: single speaker reveals character to a silent audience.
- **Allegory**: story with a double meaning, characters/events symbolize ideas.
