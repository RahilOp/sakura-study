## Definitions
- **Caste**: ascribed, endogamous, hierarchical social group.
- **Varna**: four-fold classical division – Brahmin, Kshatriya, Vaishya, Shudra.
- **Joint family**: extended family living and pooling resources together.
- **Endogamy**: marriage within one's own group.
- **Karma and dharma**: key concepts in Hindu social thought.
