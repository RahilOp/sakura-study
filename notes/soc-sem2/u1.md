## Definitions
- **Village**: small, agriculture-based community with primary relationships.
- **Town/City**: larger, non-agricultural, heterogeneous population.
- **Rural-urban continuum**: gradual transition between village and city life.
- **Unity in diversity**: India's hallmark of varied cultures within one nation.
