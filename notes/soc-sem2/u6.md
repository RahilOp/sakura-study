# Social classes in India

## Key Topics
- Agrarian classes: big landlords, rich peasants, middle peasants, poor peasants, agricultural labourers.
- Industrial-urban classes: capitalist/bourgeoisie, white-collar workers, proletariat, informal sector.
- Middle class: salaried professionals, small businessmen, educated service holders.
- Exclusion: marginalised groups denied access to resources, rights and opportunities.
- Inclusion: policies and processes that bring excluded groups into mainstream society.
- Social mobility in India is shaped by education, reservation, urbanisation and migration.
- Class and caste often overlap but are analytically distinct forms of stratification.

## Definitions
- **Social stratification**: hierarchical arrangement of individuals/groups.
- **Bourgeoisie**: capitalist class owning means of production.
- **Proletariat**: working class selling labour.
- **Informal sector**: unregulated, low-wage employment.
