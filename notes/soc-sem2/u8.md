## Definitions
- **National integration**: unity among diverse groups within a nation.
- **Casteism**: prejudice/discrimination based on caste.
- **Communalism**: politics based on religious identity.
- **Naxalism**: left-wing extremist movement rooted in agrarian distress.
- **Regionalism**: promoting interests of a particular region over national unity.
