## Definitions
- **Cultural diversity**: variety of languages, religions, customs in India.
- **Ethnic diversity**: presence of multiple ethnic/racial groups.
- **Caste**: hereditary social stratification based on birth.
- **Religious diversity**: Hinduism, Islam, Christianity, Sikhism, Buddhism, Jainism, etc.
