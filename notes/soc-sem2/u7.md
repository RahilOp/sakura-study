# Population

## Key Topics
- India is the second most populous country; 2011 census population ~1.21 billion.
- Population growth is determined by birth rate, death rate and migration.
- Demographic transition: shift from high birth/death rates to low birth/death rates.
- Age structure: large young population; declining dependency ratio expected.
- Sex ratio: number of females per 1000 males (India's is low).
- Population policy: family planning, education, women's empowerment, health services.
- Census of India is conducted every 10 years; next was due 2021.

## Definitions
- **Birth rate**: live births per 1000 population per year.
- **Death rate**: deaths per 1000 population per year.
- **Migration**: movement of people across regions/countries.
- **Demographic dividend**: economic growth potential from a large working-age population.
