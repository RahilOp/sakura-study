# Tribal communities in India

## Key Topics
- Scheduled Tribes (STs) comprise about 8.6% of India's population (2011 census).
- Largest ST populations: Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Odisha, Rajasthan, Gujarat.
- Highest ST percentage: Lakshadweep, Mizoram, Nagaland, Meghalaya, Arunachal Pradesh.
- Major tribes: Gond, Bhil, Santhal, Munda, Oraon, Ho, Khasi, Garo, Naga, Bodo, Warli.
- Tribes practice shifting cultivation (jhum), terraced farming, hunting-gathering, pastoralism.
- Issues: land alienation, displacement, lack of education/healthcare, identity loss.
- Constitutional safeguards: Fifth and Sixth Schedules, ST status, reservations.

## Definitions
- **Adivasi**: indigenous/tribal people of India.
- **Shifting cultivation (Jhum)**: slash-and-burn agriculture.
- **Assimilation**: tribal groups adopting dominant culture.
- **Integration**: tribes participating in mainstream while retaining identity.
