# Normal distribution and significance

## Key Topics
- Normal distribution is bell-shaped, symmetric around the mean; mean = median = mode.
- 68% of data lies within ±1 SD, 95% within ±2 SD, 99.7% within ±3 SD.
- Significance level (alpha) is the probability of rejecting a true null hypothesis.
- Common alpha values: 0.05, 0.01, 0.001.
- t-test compares means of two groups or a sample mean to a known value.
- One-tailed test predicts direction; two-tailed test predicts difference either way.
- p-value < alpha means result is statistically significant.

## Definitions
- **Null hypothesis (H0)**: no effect or no difference.
- **Alternative hypothesis (H1)**: predicted effect or difference.
- **Type I error**: rejecting a true null hypothesis.
- **Type II error**: failing to reject a false null hypothesis.
