## Definitions
- **Hypothesis**: testable statement predicting a relationship between variables.
- **Variables**: independent (cause) and dependent (effect).
- **Sampling**: selecting a representative subset from a population.
- **Experimental method**: controlled study of cause and effect.
- **Observation method**: watching and recording behaviour.
