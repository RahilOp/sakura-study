## Definitions
- **Nominal scale**: categories without order (e.g. gender, religion).
- **Ordinal scale**: ranked order but unequal intervals.
- **Interval scale**: equal intervals but no true zero (e.g. temperature).
- **Ratio scale**: equal intervals and true zero (e.g. income, weight).
