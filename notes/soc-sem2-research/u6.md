# Validity and reliability

## Key Topics
- Validity: whether a test measures what it claims to measure.
- Types of validity: content, criterion-related, construct, face validity.
- Reliability: consistency of measurement across time/items/raters.
- Types of reliability: test-retest, split-half, inter-rater, internal consistency.
- A test can be reliable without being valid, but valid tests are usually reliable.
- Standardisation ensures uniform administration and scoring.

## Definitions
- **Validity**: accuracy of measurement.
- **Reliability**: consistency of measurement.
- **Content validity**: coverage of the subject domain.
