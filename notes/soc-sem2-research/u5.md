# Correlation and Chi-Square tests

## Key Topics
- Correlation measures the strength and direction of relationship between two variables.
- Pearson's r: linear correlation for interval/ratio data (-1 to +1).
- Spearman's rho: rank correlation for ordinal data.
- Positive correlation: both variables increase together.
- Negative correlation: one variable increases while the other decreases.
- Correlation does not imply causation.
- Chi-Square test checks association between categorical variables.
- Expected frequency should be at least 5 for most Chi-Square tests.

## Definitions
- **Correlation coefficient**: numerical measure of correlation.
- **Chi-Square (χ²)**: non-parametric test of independence.
- **Degrees of freedom**: (rows - 1) × (columns - 1) in Chi-Square.
