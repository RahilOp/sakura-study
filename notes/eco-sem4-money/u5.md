## Definitions
- **Public finance**: revenue, expenditure and debt of government.
- **Public goods**: non-rival and non-excludable (e.g. national defence).
- **Budget**: annual statement of estimated receipts and expenditure.
- **Fiscal policy**: use of government spending and taxation to influence economy.
