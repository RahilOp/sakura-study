# Unit 3

## Key Topics
- UNIT- V: Central Banking System
- Central Banking: Meaning and functions of Central bank; Reserve Bank of India (RBI):
- Objectives, functions and methods of credit control: quantitative and qualitative methods;
- Indian money market: components and limitations.

## Definitions
- **RBI**: Reserve Bank of India, established 1935, nationalised 1949.
- **Quantitative credit control**: bank rate, CRR, SLR, open market operations.
- **Qualitative credit control**: margin requirements, moral suasion, selective credit controls.
- **Money market**: market for short-term funds.
