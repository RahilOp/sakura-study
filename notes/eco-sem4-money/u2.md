## Definitions
- **M1, M2, M3, M4**: measures of money supply in India.
- **High-powered money**: currency with public + bank reserves (C + RR + ER).
- **Credit creation**: commercial banks create deposits through lending.
- **Nationalisation of banks**: 14 major banks on 19 July 1969.
