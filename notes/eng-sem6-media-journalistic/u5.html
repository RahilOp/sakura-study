William Shakespeare — Macbeth

Context

  • Macbeth is one of Shakespeare's great tragedies.
  • It explores ambition, guilt, fate, and the corrupting effect of power.

Plot

  • Macbeth, a Scottish general, is told by three witches that he will become king.
  • Encouraged by his wife Lady Macbeth, he murders King Duncan and seizes the throne.
  • He becomes a tyrant and kills more people to protect his power.
  • Eventually he is defeated by Macduff and Malcolm; Lady Macbeth goes mad and dies.

Themes

  • Ambition: unchecked ambition leads to destruction.
  • Guilt: Macbeth and Lady Macbeth suffer psychologically.
  • Fate vs free will: do the witches predict or cause events?
  • Kingship and tyranny: legitimate rule vs bloody usurpation.
  • The supernatural: witches, visions, ghosts.

Key symbols

  • Blood: guilt and murder.
  • Darkness: evil and deception.
  • The dagger: temptation and hallucination.

Vishal Bhardwaj's Maqbool (2003)

Adaptation

  • Vishal Bhardwaj directed Maqbool, a Hindi film that transposes Macbeth to the Mumbai underworld.
  • Maqbool (Macbeth) is the right-hand man of underworld don Abbaji (Duncan).
  • Nimmi (Lady Macbeth) is Abbaji's mistress and secretly loves Maqbool.

Comparison

Aspect Macbeth Maqbool
Setting Medieval Scotland Mumbai underworld
Macbeth Maqbool, gangster Ambitious lieutenant
Lady Macbeth Nimmi Don's mistress
Duncan Abbaji Underworld don
Witches Two corrupt police officers Predictions and manipulation
Banquo Guddu Killed by Maqbool
Macduff Inspector Avenger

Themes translated

  • Ambition and betrayal within a crime family.
  • Guilt and supernatural dread replaced by underworld tension.
  • Love and lust complicate the Macbeth-Lady Macbeth dynamic.

Exam points

  • Maqbool is Vishal Bhardwaj's adaptation of Macbeth.
  • Maqbool = Macbeth; Nimmi = Lady Macbeth; Abbaji = Duncan.
  • Setting: Mumbai underworld.
  • The witches are replaced by corrupt police officers.

Analysing the adaptation

What is preserved?

  • The arc of ambition, murder, guilt, and downfall.
  • The close but toxic relationship between Macbeth and Lady Macbeth.
  • The theme that illegitimate power destroys.

What is changed?

  • Setting, language, names, and cultural context.
  • The supernatural witches become human corrupt cops.
  • The political tragedy becomes a gangster tragedy.

MCQ focus

  • Director: Vishal Bhardwaj.
  • Source: Macbeth.
  • Maqbool corresponds to Macbeth.
  • Nimmi corresponds to Lady Macbeth.
  • Setting: Mumbai underworld.
