## Definitions
- **Mass communication**: transmitting information to large, heterogeneous audiences.
- **Journalism**: collecting, verifying and presenting news.
- **Misinformation**: false information spread without intent to harm.
- **Disinformation**: deliberately false information spread to deceive.
