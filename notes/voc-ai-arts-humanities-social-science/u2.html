Data Analysis and Visualization for Non-Technical Fields

AI and Data

  • Data = facts, numbers, text, images or sounds used by AI.
  • Structured data = organised in rows and columns (e.g., spreadsheets).
  • Unstructured data = text, images, audio, video without a fixed format.
  • Humanities data is often unstructured: poems, speeches, court records, interviews.

AI-Powered Analysis

  • Tools that non-technical users can use:
  • Google Sheets Explore = automatic charts and insights from spreadsheet data.
  • Tableau Public = drag-and-drop data visualisation.
  • These tools help find patterns without writing code.

Text Analysis in Humanities and Law

  • Sentiment analysis = detecting positive, negative or neutral tone in text.
  • Topic modeling = discovering main themes across a large collection of documents.
  • Applications:
  • Analysing public opinion in social media
  • Studying themes in literature
  • Reviewing large volumes of legal documents

Data Visualization

  • Visual forms: bar charts, line graphs, pie charts, maps, word clouds.
  • AI tools can suggest the best chart type for your data.
  • Good visualisation makes complex information easier to understand and present.
