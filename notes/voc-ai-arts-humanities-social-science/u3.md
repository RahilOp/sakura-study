# AI for Creative and Professional Projects

## Generative AI in the Arts

- Generative AI creates new content from existing patterns.
- Uses in arts:
  - Storytelling and script writing
  - Art creation and image generation
  - Music composition and sound design
- Helps artists explore variations quickly, but raises questions about authorship.

## AI in Legal Workflows

- Legal professionals use AI for:
  - Automating contract review
  - Legal research and case-law search
  - Preparing case summaries
- Benefits: faster document review, fewer missed details.
- Risks: AI can make errors; human lawyers must verify outputs.

## Creative Writing with AI

- AI can co-write:
  - Scripts and screenplays
  - Poems and lyrics
  - Essays and articles
- Best used as a writing partner for drafts, outlines and edits.

## Multimedia Production

- AI tools can help create:
  - Videos from scripts or images
  - Podcasts with voice generation and editing
  - Presentations with auto-generated slides and visuals
- Useful for students, educators, journalists and content creators.
