# Introduction to AI Tools

## AI in Practice

- **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** = computer systems that can perform tasks that normally need human intelligence.
- Common AI tools for arts and humanities:
  - **ChatGPT** = conversational AI for answering questions, drafting text, brainstorming.
  - **DALL·E** = generates images from text descriptions.
  - **Grammarly** = checks grammar, tone and clarity in writing.
- AI can speed up research, writing, editing and idea generation.

## Prompt Engineering

- A **prompt** is the instruction or question you give to an AI.
- Effective prompts are:
  - Clear and specific
  - Provide context
  - State the desired format or length
  - Include examples when needed
- Good prompt example: "Summarise the causes of the French Revolution in 5 bullet points for a BA student."

## AI for Research and Writing

- **Summarisation** = condense long articles, chapters or reports.
- **Translation** = convert text between languages while keeping meaning.
- **Research assistance** = find relevant sources, frame arguments, compare viewpoints.
- Always verify AI outputs against reliable sources.

## Creative Content Generation

- AI can generate:
  - Visuals (art, illustrations, concept images)
  - Ideas (story plots, essay topics)
  - Text (poems, scripts, social media captions)
- Useful for brainstorming, but human judgment is needed for quality and originality.
