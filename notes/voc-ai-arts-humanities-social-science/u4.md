# Building and Customizing AI Models Without Coding

## No-Code Platforms

- **No-code AI** lets users build AI applications without programming.
- Examples:
  - **Teachable Machine** = train image, sound or pose models in the browser.
  - **Runway ML** = creative AI tools for video and image editing.
  - **Chatbot.com** = build chatbots using a visual interface.

## Building Custom Models

- Steps to train a basic classifier:
  1. Collect examples (text, images or sounds).
  2. Label them into categories.
  3. Train the model using a no-code tool.
  4. Test with new examples and improve.
- Use cases: sorting documents, recognising art styles, classifying audio clips.

## Chatbot Development

- A **chatbot** is a program that simulates conversation.
- No-code chatbots can answer FAQs in:
  - Law (common legal queries)
  - Arts (course information)
  - Education (admission details)
- They save time and provide 24/7 support.

## AI Fine-Tuning

- **Fine-tuning** = adjusting a pre-trained model for a specific task.
- Example: adapting a general language model to write in a particular author's style.
- Even without coding, some platforms let users refine behaviour through prompts, examples and feedback.
