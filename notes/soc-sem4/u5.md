# Development: economic, social and human

## Key Topics
- Development implies improvement in economic, social and human conditions.
- Economic development: growth in income, production and employment.
- Social development: improvement in education, health, gender equality and social inclusion.
- Human development: expanding people's choices and capabilities (Sen, UNDP).
- HDI measures life expectancy, education and per capita income.
- Sustainable development meets present needs without compromising future generations.
- Millennium Development Goals (MDGs) and Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) guide global development.

## Definitions
- **Development**: qualitative and quantitative improvement in living standards.
- **HDI**: Human Development Index.
- **Capability approach**: Amartya Sen's focus on freedoms and opportunities.
