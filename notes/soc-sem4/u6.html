Theories of development

Key Topics

  • Modernisation theory (Smelser, Lerner, Rostow): societies progress from traditional to modern stages.
  • Rostow's stages: traditional society, preconditions for take-off, take-off, drive to maturity, age of high mass consumption.
  • Dependency theory: underdevelopment results from exploitation by core capitalist countries.
  • Centre-periphery model: core countries dominate peripheral economies.
  • Globalisation: increasing integration of economies, cultures and societies worldwide.
  • Critics argue development theories often ignore local contexts, culture and power relations.

Definitions

  • Modernisation: transition from traditional to modern industrial society.
  • Dependency: underdevelopment caused by external exploitation.
  • Centre-periphery: global inequality between dominant core and dependent periphery.
