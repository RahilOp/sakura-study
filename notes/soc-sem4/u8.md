# Ecology and development

## Key Topics
- Unit 3 : DEVELOPMENTAL: Development induced displacement, ecological
- degradation, consumerism, crisis of Values.

## Definitions
- **Development-induced displacement**: people forced out by projects.
- **Rehabilitation**: restoring livelihoods of displaced persons.
- **Sustainable development**: balancing growth with environmental protection.
- **Climate change**: long-term shifts in temperature and weather patterns.
