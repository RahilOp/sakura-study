## Definitions
- **White-collar crime**: illegal acts by respectable persons in occupational roles.
- **Cyber crime**: criminal activities using computers/networks.
- **Drug addiction**: compulsive dependence on psychoactive substances.
- **Terrorism**: use of violence to create fear for political/ideological goals.
