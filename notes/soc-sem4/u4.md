# Familial problems: dowry, domestic violence, divorce

## Family as an institution

- The family is a primary social institution responsible for socialisation, emotional support, and economic cooperation.
- It is also a site of power, conflict, and inequality.
- Familial problems arise when relationships within the family become strained or abusive.

---

## Dowry

### Meaning
- **Dowry** is the transfer of goods, money, or property from the bride's family to the groom's family at or before marriage.
- It is different from bride price, where the groom's family gives wealth to the bride's family.

### Problems associated
- Financial burden on the bride's family.
- Harassment and violence if dowry demands are not met.
- **Dowry deaths**: deaths of brides due to dowry-related harassment.
- Reinforces son preference and gender inequality.

### Legal responses
- **Dowry Prohibition Act, 1961**: prohibits giving and taking dowry.
- Protection against domestic violence under various laws.

### Social responses
- Education and awareness.
- Encouraging dowry-free marriages.
- Women's empowerment and economic independence.

---

## Domestic violence

### Meaning
- **Domestic violence** is abuse that occurs within the household, typically by one family member against another.
- Forms: physical, sexual, emotional, verbal, and economic abuse.

### Causes
- Patriarchal norms and gender inequality.
- Alcohol and substance abuse.
- Economic stress and unemployment.
- Lack of legal awareness and support systems.

### Effects
- Physical injuries and mental health problems.
- Social isolation and loss of confidence.
- Impact on children's well-being.

### Legal responses
- **Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005**.
- Helplines, shelters, and counselling services.
- Police and judicial support.

---

## Divorce

### Meaning
- **Divorce** is the legal dissolution of marriage.
- It ends the marital relationship and usually resolves issues of custody, maintenance, and property.

### Causes
- Incompatibility and communication breakdown.
- Domestic violence and abuse.
- Dowry harassment.
- Adultery, desertion, and mental cruelty.
- Changing gender roles and economic independence of women.

### Trends
- Divorce rates are rising in urban India.
- Social stigma around divorce is declining but still significant.
- Different personal laws govern divorce for different religions.

---

## Intra-generational and inter-generational conflict

### Intra-generational conflict
- Conflict between members of the same generation, such as siblings or spouses.
- Often over property, inheritance, or authority.

### Inter-generational conflict
- Conflict between different generations, such as parents and children.
- Caused by differences in values, lifestyle, education, and career choices.
- Common in rapidly changing societies.

---

## Problems of the elderly

### Issues
- Neglect and isolation.
- Lack of financial security.
- Health problems and inadequate care.
- Loss of authority in nuclear families.

### Responses
- **Maintenance and Welfare of Parents and Senior Citizens Act, 2007**.
- Old-age pensions and healthcare schemes.
- Old-age homes and community support.
- Promoting respect and care for elders.

## Exam checklist

- Dowry = transfer from bride's to groom's family; linked to harassment and deaths.
- Domestic violence includes physical, sexual, emotional, and economic abuse.
- Divorce = legal end of marriage; rising in urban India.
- Intra-generational = same generation; inter-generational = different generations.
- Elderly face neglect, insecurity, and health problems.
