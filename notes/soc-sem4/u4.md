# Familial problems: dowry, domestic violence, divorce

## Key Topics
- Unit 2 : FAMILIAL: Dowry, domestic violence, divorce, intra and inter-
- Generational conflict, problemes of elderly.

## Definitions
- **Dowry**: transfer of goods/money from bride's family to groom's family.
- **Domestic violence**: abuse within the household.
- **Divorce**: legal dissolution of marriage.
- **Inter-generational conflict**: tension between age groups over values/resources.
