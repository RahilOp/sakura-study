# Issues of development

## Key Topics
- Agrarian crisis: farmer indebtedness, land fragmentation, low prices, crop failure, suicides.
- Human resource development: education, skill training and health as drivers of growth.
- Unemployment: open, disguised, seasonal, structural and frictional.
- Disguised unemployment: more workers than required, marginal product is zero.
- Government responses: MGNREGA, skill missions, rural development programmes.
- Migration from rural to urban areas creates informal settlements and slums.

## Definitions
- **Agrarian crisis**: structural distress in farming sector.
- **Human capital**: skills, education and health of workforce.
- **Disguised unemployment**: hidden underemployment in agriculture.
