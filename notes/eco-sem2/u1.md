## Definitions
- **Macroeconomics**: study of economy as a whole.
- **GDP**: market value of all final goods/services produced within a country in a year.
- **GNP**: GDP + net factor income from abroad.
- **NNP**: GNP minus depreciation.
- **Circular flow of income**: movement of money between households, firms, government and foreign sector.
