## Definitions
- **Inflation**: sustained rise in general price level.
- **Demand-pull inflation**: excess demand.
- **Cost-push inflation**: rising input costs.
- **Phillips curve**: short-run inverse relation between inflation and unemployment.
- **Stagflation**: high inflation + high unemployment + stagnation.
