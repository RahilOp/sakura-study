## Definitions
- **Multiplier**: k = 1/(1-MPC) = 1/MPS.
- **Accelerator**: investment induced by change in consumption/output.
- **Liquidity preference**: demand for money (transactions, precautionary, speculative).
- **Liquidity trap**: interest rates near zero, monetary policy ineffective.
- **IS curve**: goods-market equilibrium; **LM curve**: money-market equilibrium.
