## Definitions
- **APC**: Average Propensity to Consume = C/Y.
- **MPC**: Marginal Propensity to Consume = ΔC/ΔY.
- **MPS + MPC = 1**.
- **Autonomous investment**: independent of income level.
- **Induced investment**: changes with income/output.
