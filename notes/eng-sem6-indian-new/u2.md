# Poetry I

## Indian English poetry: an overview

- Indian English poetry blends Indian themes, myths, landscapes, and social concerns with English forms.
- Poets in this unit explore identity, history, mythology, family, poverty, and gender.
- Exam focus: identify the poet, the theme, and the Indian context of each poem.

---

## Toru Dutt — Lakshman

### Context
- **Toru Dutt** was a 19th-century Indian English poet and novelist.
- She is one of the earliest Indian women writers in English.
- "Lakshman" retells an episode from the **Ramayana**.

### Theme
- The poem dramatises **Lakshman's loyalty** to Rama and Sita during their forest exile.
- It often focuses on Lakshman's devotion and the emotional cost of exile.
- Uses Indian mythological material in English verse.

### Significance
- Shows early Indian English poetry's engagement with Indian classics.
- Dutt's work is important for the development of Indian English literature.

---

## Nissim Ezekiel — A Poem of Dedication

### Context
- **Nissim Ezekiel** is a foundational figure in modern Indian English poetry.
- His poetry is known for irony, restraint, and urban Indian experience.

### Theme
- "A Poem of Dedication" reflects the poet's commitment to his craft and country.
- It often expresses a complex, critical love for India.
- Ezekiel's tone is typically self-aware and quietly ironic.

### Style
- Clear, controlled language.
- Blends personal feeling with social observation.
- Avoids excessive romanticism.

---

## Jayanta Mahapatra — Hunger

### Context
- **Jayanta Mahapatra** was an Odia-Indian English poet.
- "Hunger" is one of his most famous and disturbing poems.

### Theme
- The poem explores poverty, exploitation, and the commodification of the female body.
- A fisherman offers his daughter to a stranger because of desperate poverty.
- "Hunger" is both physical and moral.

### Key features
- Stark realism.
- Imagery of coastal Odisha.
- Social critique without explicit preaching.

### Exam points
- "Hunger" addresses **poverty and sexual exploitation**.
- Mahapatra often writes about **Odisha**, its landscape and people.
- The poem is known for its unflinching realism.

---

## Keki N. Daruwala — Mother

### Context
- **Keki N. Daruwala** was an Indian English poet and short-story writer.
- His poetry often draws on history, myth, and personal memory.

### Theme
- "Mother" is a personal poem about the poet's relationship with his mother.
- It explores memory, loss, love, and the passage of time.
- The mother figure may also carry symbolic or cultural associations.

### Style
- Reflective and lyrical.
- Use of imagery and controlled emotion.
