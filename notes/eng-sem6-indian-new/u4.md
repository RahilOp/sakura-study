# Fiction I

## Key Topics
- Kamala Markandaya - Nectar in a Sieve
- Mulk Raj Anand - Untouchable

## Definitions
- **Kamala Markandaya**: 'Nectar in a Sieve' depicts rural poverty and resilience.
- **Mulk Raj Anand**: 'Untouchable' follows a Dalit protagonist's day of humiliation.
