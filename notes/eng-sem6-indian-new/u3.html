Drama I

Indian English drama: an overview

  • Indian English drama often engages with social issues such as gender, sexuality, caste, religion, and identity.
  • Playwrights in this unit use realistic and symbolic techniques to explore marginalised experiences.
  • Exam focus: identify the playwright, the social issue, and the dramatic technique.

Mahesh Dattani — Seven Steps Around the Fire

Context

  • Mahesh Dattani is a leading contemporary Indian playwright writing in English.
  • He is known for tackling taboo subjects in urban Indian society.
  • Seven Steps Around the Fire focuses on the hijra community and gender identity.

Plot and theme

  • The play involves the murder of a young hijra and the investigation that follows.
  • It exposes police brutality, social stigma, family rejection, and the violence faced by transgender people.
  • The "seven steps" echo the saat phere of Hindu marriage, ironically framing the hijra's excluded position.

Key concerns

  • Gender and sexuality: the play challenges binary ideas of male and female.
  • Marginalisation: hijras are pushed to the edges of society and survive through begging and sex work.
  • Justice and silence: the legal and social systems fail to protect vulnerable lives.

Dramatic technique

  • Multiple time frames and locations.
  • Direct address and chorus-like narration.
  • Mix of realism and stylised performance.

Exam points

  • Dattani's play centres on the hijra community.
  • It critiques social stigma, police violence, and gender norms.
  • "Seven steps" alludes to the Hindu marriage ritual.

Asif Currimbhoy — The Doldrummers

Context

  • Asif Currimbhoy was one of the most prolific Indian English playwrights.
  • His plays cover politics, religion, social conflict, and personal relationships.
  • The Doldrummers explores stagnation, middle-class life, and emotional emptiness.

Theme

  • "Doldrums" means a state of inactivity or stagnation.
  • The play examines people caught in routine, unfulfilled relationships, and spiritual dryness.
  • It reflects urban Indian anxieties and the search for meaning.

Style

  • Currimbhoy's dramatic style is often poetic and symbolic.
  • He uses interior monologue and expressionist devices.

Exam points

  • Currimbhoy wrote prolifically in Indian English drama.
  • The Doldrummers deals with stagnation and emotional emptiness.
  • His style blends realism with poetic/expressionist elements.
