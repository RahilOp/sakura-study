## Definitions
- **Hind Swaraj**: Gandhi's 1909 text on self-rule and passive resistance.
- **Passive Resistance**: non-violent opposition to unjust laws.
- **The Other Side of Silence**: Urvashi Butalia's oral history of Partition.
