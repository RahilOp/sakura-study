Stress and Psychosomatic Disorders

Nature of Stress

  • Stress = any event or demand that requires the person to adjust.
  • Stressors = sources of stress: life events, daily hassles, catastrophes, chronic strains.
  • Eustress = positive, motivating stress.
  • Distress = negative, harmful stress.

Types and Sources of Stress

  • Physical stressors: illness, injury, noise, pollution.
  • Psychological stressors: frustration, conflict, pressure.
  • Social stressors: relationship problems, financial issues, discrimination.
  • Major life events: bereavement, divorce, exams, unemployment.

Trauma- and Stressor-Related Disorders

  • PTSD (Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder) = after a traumatic event: flashbacks, nightmares, avoidance, hyperarousal.
  • Acute Stress Disorder = similar symptoms but lasts 3 days to 1 month after trauma.
  • Both are linked to extreme fear, helplessness or horror.

Coping with Stress

  • Problem-focused coping = tackling the source of stress.
  • Emotion-focused coping = managing emotional reaction.
  • Healthy coping: exercise, social support, relaxation, time management.
  • Unhealthy coping: substance use, avoidance, denial.

Psychosomatic Disorders

  • Physical symptoms caused or worsened by psychological factors.
  • Examples: hypertension, peptic ulcer, asthma, migraine, irritable bowel syndrome.
  • Treatment: medical care plus stress management, CBT, biofeedback.
